Privacy policy

Your data on Sous 30

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Sous 30 is a free site, made by Daniel, Nicolas and Samuel, that lists Steam deals under €30. There is no advertising and no selling of data. You can browse every deal without creating an account.

What the site stores if you create an account

  • Signing up by email: your email address and your password. The password is kept in encrypted form; we cannot read it.
  • Signing in with Discord or Google: that service gives us your name or username, your email address and your profile picture. We never receive your Discord or Google password.
  • Your favourites: the list of games you marked with a heart.
  • The “Email alerts” setting: yes or no. It stays on “No” until you turn it on.

This information is only used to recognise you when you come back and to keep your favourites on all your devices.

Emails the site may send you

If you turn on “Email alerts” in your account menu, the site writes to you when one of your favourites is back on sale. These emails are sent from a Gmail address belonging to the project, and are currently written in French. You can stop them at any time by switching the setting back to “No”. Without this setting, the only email you can get is the one you ask for with “Forgot password?”.

Where this data is kept

Accounts are handled by Supabase, a specialised service, on servers located in the European Union. Your browser also keeps a small session file so that you stay signed in; it is removed when you sign out. It also remembers your language and which deals you have already seen, to show you what is new.

Other services your visit contacts

To display the page, your browser talks to GitHub Pages (site hosting), Google Fonts (typefaces), jsDelivr (a code file) and Steam's servers (game images). As with any website, these services see your IP address. Sous 30 sets no advertising cookies and does not track your browsing.

Deleting your account or asking a question

You can ask at any time for your account and favourites to be deleted, or ask a question, by opening a request on the project page on GitHub. Only give the email address or username of the account, never your password.

Steam

Sous 30 is an independent project, not affiliated with Valve. The links on the cards lead to the Steam store, which has its own privacy rules.
